Confirm the licence shown on each source page before downloading. Freesound
may require sign-in. Upload the original downloads and a screenshot or copy
of each source page's licence notice. For a home recording, include who made
it and confirm permission to publish it in this public repository. Avoid
recording voices or other identifiable background audio.

Once verified and auditioned, encode edited excerpts as mono MP3 at 44.1 kHz.
Record the actual source licence and edits here and enable only the delivered
filenames in `bathtubRecordings` in `foley.js`. Beds should be several seconds
long with consistent friction. The engine applies a 200 ms overlap to each bed.
